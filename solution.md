# ManaKirana AI
### AI-Powered Voice-Based Inventory & Grocery Management

**The vision:** Making neighbourhood kirana stores smarter through voice AI and automation.

## Problem

Many local kirana stores still manage customer orders, product quantities, inventory and pricing manually. Handling different products, units and customer requirements can be time-consuming and may lead to calculation errors or inventory mismatches.

## Proposed Solution

ManaKirana AI is an AI-powered solution designed to simplify and automate everyday kirana store operations.

Shopkeepers can provide grocery requirements through voice in Telugu. The system converts spoken requests into structured product information, identifies products and quantities, and uses predefined product catalogue details and pricing to calculate order totals.

The goal is to bring inventory management and grocery order processing together in one simple platform.

## Key Capabilities

- **Voice-Based Ordering:** Convert spoken Telugu grocery requests into structured product information.
- **AI Product Recognition:** Identify product names, quantities and units from natural-language requests.
- **Inventory Management:** Manage product quantities and inventory using predefined catalogue information.
- **Automatic Price Calculation:** Calculate order totals based on product prices, quantities and configured units.
- **Order Processing:** Simplify the process from customer requirements to a calculated grocery order.
- **Regional Language Accessibility:** Make technology easier for local shopkeepers through Telugu voice interaction.

## Technology Stack

- **Python & Flask** – Backend development
- **Gnani AI Prisma** – Telugu speech recognition
- **Google Gemini** – Natural-language understanding and product extraction
- **HTML, CSS & JavaScript** – User interface

## Expected Impact

ManaKirana AI aims to reduce manual work, simplify order processing, improve inventory visibility and minimise pricing calculation errors for neighbourhood grocery stores.

By combining voice interaction, AI-based product understanding and automated calculations, the project explores how small retail businesses can benefit from accessible AI technology.

## Project Vision

To build a simple, affordable and regional-language-first automation solution that helps traditional kirana stores move towards smarter digital operations.
